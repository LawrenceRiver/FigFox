# FigFoxBench-v3

[Request access through the Hugging Face dataset page](https://huggingface.co/datasets/FigFoxBench/FigFoxBench-v3) · [Copyright and use restrictions](figfoxbench-terms.md)

FigFoxBench supports noncommercial research evaluation of scientific-figure reconstruction. Its images come from research papers and are grouped by relative visual clutter. The Hugging Face repository is private; access requires maintainer authorization. It is not an open download.

Commercial use and model training are prohibited under the maintainers' access policy, subject to rights independently granted by the original copyright holders. The maintainers have not completed figure-level rights clearance for every image. See the [access policy](figfoxbench-terms.md) and, if authorized, the [source audit](https://huggingface.co/datasets/FigFoxBench/FigFoxBench-v3/blob/main/docs/copyright-audit.md).
